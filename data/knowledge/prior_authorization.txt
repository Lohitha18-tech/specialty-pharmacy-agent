Prior Authorization Policy

Prior authorization is required for certain specialty medications before the medication can be fulfilled.

If an insurance company requests additional clinical documentation, the case should remain pending until the required documentation is received and submitted.

Required documentation may include clinical notes, diagnosis information, previous treatment history, laboratory results, or other supporting medical information.

After the requested documentation is submitted, the prior authorization should be monitored for an insurance decision.

If prior authorization is approved, the case can proceed to the next fulfillment step.

If prior authorization is denied, the case should be reviewed to determine whether an appeal or additional documentation is appropriate.
